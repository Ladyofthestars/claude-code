# Facts and sources: "You've Never Actually Touched Anything"

Every fact and number in the narration, with where it comes from. The narration states these findings without quoting the institutions behind them; the sources are listed here. Each source was opened and the wording checked on 2 November 2026.

**Careful wording used in the narration:**
- "Never actually touched" means the atoms never meet or merge: the electrons of the two surfaces push each other apart (#2, #5–#7). The video says what you feel is that push.
- The stadium and blueberry comparison is the textbook's own (#1).
- The sugar-cube line is a calculation from sourced numbers (#13), said as "would".
