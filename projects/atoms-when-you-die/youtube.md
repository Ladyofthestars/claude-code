## Description (paste as is)

Every breath you take holds, on average, about one molecule of air from Julius Caesar's last breath. Nothing in this world really disappears, and when you die, almost none of your atoms will be destroyed.

So where do they go? In this video we follow them: into the soil, the air and the sea, into trees, animals and other people, deep into the Earth for thousands and millions of years, and finally back to the stars.

This is part two. Part one, "Almost Every Atom in You Is Older Than the Earth", is where your atoms came from.
