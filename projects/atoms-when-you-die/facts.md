# Facts and sources: "What Happens to Your Atoms When You Die?"

Every fact and number in the narration, with where it comes from. The narration states these findings without quoting the institutions behind them; the sources are listed here. Each source was opened and the wording checked on 2 November 2026. Facts carried over from video 1 ("You Are Made of Stars") keep their source from `facts.md` at the top of the repository.

**Careful wording used in the narration:**
- "Almost none of your atoms will be destroyed": the exceptions are radioactive atoms such as carbon-14 (#11–13), which slowly turn into other elements.
- "On average" for the Caesar estimate (#1); it is an average, not a promise about any single breath.
- "Many scientists think" for Earth being swallowed (#21).
- "Maybe" for atoms becoming part of new life around a future star (#24).
