# Picture prompts: "You Are Made of Stars"

**Model:** Nano Banana 2 Lite (Google Gemini) through the Gemini connector, square, 1K. GPT Image 2.5 on Higgsfield was the first choice, but the account has 0 credits on the free plan.

**Prompt pattern:** what it is and from which angle, then "lots of empty space around it, nothing touching the edges", then the style line word for word, then the ending.

- **Style line:** Black and white scientific illustration, fine ink lines and soft graphite shading, like a page from an old textbook; one single object, isolated and centered.
- **Ending:** plain white background, no text, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no shadows, no frame

**Checks on every picture:** text (looked at), colour (average saturation of the drawing above 0.12 fails), cut-off edges (drawing within 2% of the border fails), extra objects or frames (looked at), style (looked at side by side). Remakes: at most two per picture.

**Reused from "The New Earth" short, no new prompt:** earth, person, open_hand, fist (prompts in `prompts-new-earth-short.md`). All 21 pictures are redrawn in the same dark ink (#24262b) for the video.
