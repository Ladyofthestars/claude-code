# Picture prompts: The New Earth

Every prompt follows the same pattern: what the object is and the angle, then "lots of empty space around it, nothing touching the edges", then the style line from `script.md` word for word, then the ending.

You chose the midnight-blue background in the style line, so the ending leaves out "plain white background".

- **Model:** Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield, 1k resolution, 2 credits per picture. GPT Image 2.5 was the first choice, but Higgsfield only allows it on the Basic plan or higher.
- **Style line (in every prompt):** Hand-drawn illustration in fine pale cream ink lines with soft chalk-like shading, on a plain flat deep midnight-blue background, like a page from an old hand-drawn star atlas; calm and quiet; one single object, isolated and centered, nothing else in the frame, no text, no labels, no numbers, no other colors.
- **Ending (in every prompt):** No text, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no shadows, no frame.
